I'm starting a small log. About once a month I'll write down what I worked on, what went wrong, and what I learned. If I get something useful out of it, you might too.

## What you'll find here

- **Monthly notes.** A few lines on what I shipped and fixed.
- **Longer posts.** One topic at a time, like BLE pairing on Android, keeping streams in sync, or moving a screen to Compose.
- **A daily reading list.** Every morning the site pulls in new posts about AI, Android and Kotlin from sources I follow, so there's always something to read. I don't write those, and each link goes to the original.

## About the work I can't talk about

Some of my work was for clients, so I can't share their code or numbers. I'll write about the problems and the approach, not their internals.

## Say hi

If you have a question about any of this, email me. I read everything.
